# Tastemaker Knowledge Base — Index (the whole ladder)

**Two knowledge bases, one ladder.** Tastemaker evaluates *and teaches* the full climb from measurable craft to design judgment:

- **Taste principles** — `TS-NNN`, 12 entries across 11 categories, at the `kb/` root. The *judgment* layer (Characterful → Convicted rungs). Evaluated.
- **Craft rules** — `CR-NNN`, 19 entries across 6 dimensions, under [`kb/craft/`](craft/_index.md). The *measurable* layer (the Coherent rung's teachable floor). Taught, with concrete fixes + eye-training.

Both were grown against real evaluation need and iterated through repeated design critiques. TS entries use a six-section body (Definition → Why It Matters → How to Evaluate → Exemplars → When to Flex → Sources). CR entries use a six-part body (Rule → Why → How to Spot → Fix → Eye-training → When to Flex).

## The Ladder (one tool climbs all of it)

```
        How high SHOULD this climb?  ← TS-011 (Contextual Fit) sets the ceiling
        ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  4. CONVICTED    — commits to a point of view; feels inevitable      ┐
  3. CHARACTERFUL — expresses a specific, earned identity             ├─ TASTE  (TS-NNN, judgment — EVALUATE)
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  2. COHERENT     — the parts agree; it's finished; it has rhythm     ┐
     └─ the measurable craft floor of Coherent ──────────────────────┤── CRAFT (CR-NNN, measurable — TEACH)
  1. CORRECT      — usable and clear   ← ux-critique's floor; assumed │  (spacing, type, color, shadow,
                                                                       ┘   alignment, density)
```

**The split that matters:** the **lower rungs are *taught*** (craft is rule-based, measurable — a finding is "8 type sizes, cap to 5, here's how to see it next time"), the **upper rungs are *evaluated*** (taste is judgment — a finding is "this commits to no point of view"). One tool, one ladder, but a craft finding stays mechanically precise and a taste finding stays a judgment call — **the rungs keep the precision separate even inside one tool.** Craft is *how well it's made*; taste is *whether it's good*. You climb craft first, then taste.

Each rung assumes the one below it. **Grade = how close a design gets to its context-appropriate ceiling**, not its absolute rung. Higher is not better; *right for the context* is better.

## By Rung

### CORRECT (rung 1) — assumed, not evaluated
The usability/clarity floor. ux-critique's domain. Tastemaker assumes it and does not re-litigate it.

### CRAFT FLOOR (the measurable base of Coherent) — *taught, not just judged*
The 19 `CR-NNN` rules — spacing, type, color, shadow, alignment, density — are the measurable craft beneath Coherence. **Full sub-index: [`kb/craft/_index.md`](craft/_index.md).** These are *taught* (fix + rule + eye-training), not evaluated as judgment. A design that's incoherent in its craft (off-scale spacing, 8 type sizes, failing contrast) can't reach the taste rungs above — tighten craft first.

## Coverage Status

All 11 categories have at least one entry (Material Honesty has two in the motion sub-domain). Future entries should be promoted only when repeated critiques reveal a durable gap.

### Standing rule: exemplar diversity (anti-monoculture)

**Every principle must draw exemplars from at least three distinct design traditions.** This is the structural answer to the strongest critique of taste codification: a shared reference can manufacture the same convergence-to-average it claims to fight. Tastemaker rewards divergence, but that defense only holds if the exemplars are not all one tribe. A KB that praises only one design tradition would quietly nudge every design toward that aesthetic.

**In practice:** exemplars should span editorial, consumer-warm, dense professional, utilitarian, and developer-tool traditions. The point of view is opinionated about restraint and conviction on purpose; the examples must remain pluralist so that opinion does not harden into a single look.

**Motion (Material Honesty):** TS-004 (Motion as Meaning) and TS-012 (Direct Manipulation as Material) cover motion *taste*. Motion *craft*—interruptibility, velocity preservation, layout transitions, resistance, and reduced-motion implementation—belongs to the Correct-rung floor and requires a dedicated motion or usability review.
