# Convention over Expression on High-Stakes, Low-Frequency Tasks

## Ranking

**On tasks people do rarely and cannot afford to get wrong, follow the convention they already know, even when an expressive treatment would be more distinctive and more on-brand.**

## Why

TS-008 (Type as First Material) and the wider pull toward brand expression reward identity and voice. TS-011 (Convention as the Tasteful Choice) says some surfaces should stop at Coherent. On a payment confirmation, a password reset or a tax form, these collide: the brand team wants the moment to feel like the product, and the user wants it to feel like every other safe version of that task they have seen. TS-009 (Register-Stakes Match) sits on the convention side here: high stakes call for a calm register.

The ranking sides with convention because the user arrives at these moments anxious and unpracticed. They do the task too rarely to learn a new pattern, and the cost of a mistake is high. A familiar shape lowers the effort of trusting the screen. The cost accepted is a less distinctive moment and a brand voice turned down to quiet. That is the correct place for it.

## How to Spot the Conflict

- A high-stakes flow uses custom controls where platform controls exist.
- Expressive type, motion or illustration appears on a step where users enter money, credentials or legal consent.
- The critique would praise character (TS-008) on a surface whose ceiling is Coherent (TS-011).
- A redesign of a rarely used, high-risk flow is justified by brand consistency alone.

Cite TR-004 and grade against the Coherent ceiling.

## Exemplars

**Utilitarian (government service design systems).** Public-service design systems standardize forms, errors and confirmation pages across thousands of services. The pages are plain on purpose; users who file once a year recognize the shape immediately.

**Commerce (checkout).** Widely used checkouts converge on the same shape: summary, payment, one emphasized pay action, clear totals. Distinctiveness lives elsewhere in the store.

**Financial (transfer confirmation).** A money transfer's final step shows amount, recipient and timing in the plainest available form. The brand shows up in the reliability, not the styling.

## When It Flips

- **Frequency:** a high-stakes task done daily (a trader's order ticket, a nurse's charting) becomes practiced. Expert efficiency and earned density can outrank convention there.
- **Identity surfaces:** onboarding, heroes and the core loop are where expression belongs. TR-004 applies only where stakes are high and frequency is low.
- **Bad conventions:** a convention that is itself confusing or deceptive is not protected by this ranking. Correctness wins first.
